Input / output contract

OUTPUT 01

OUTPUT 02

The working product produces notation and MIDI; this public demo exports only its edited JSON fixture.

Pipeline walkthrough

Seven stages, seven places to observe or recover.

  1. 01 · Preprocess

    Decode to a stable sample rate, normalize channels, and preserve source metadata.

  2. 02 · Separate

    Isolate the percussion signal locally so harmonic content does not dominate detection.

  3. 03 · Detect

    Infer kick, snare, and cymbal events with timestamps and confidence values.

  4. 04 · Quantize

    Map continuous events to musical subdivisions while retaining timing evidence.

  5. 05 · Arrange

    Group hits into measures and resolve conflicts without mutating previous stages.

  6. 06 · Notate

    Translate the score model into an editable visual grid and player timeline.

  7. 07 · Export

    Serialize practice artifacts and diagnostics with source/version provenance.

Design decisions

Playback is a view over the score, not a second source of truth.

Audio clock over animation clock

Sounds are scheduled against `AudioContext.currentTime`; visual state follows the same step index.

Gesture-gated audio

The audio context is created only after Play, respecting browser policy and avoiding surprise sound.

Immutable editing

Arrangement presets and individual edits create new arrays, keeping reset and export deterministic.

Privacy boundary

No upload input, network request, model download, commercial recording, lyrics, or analytics exists here.
